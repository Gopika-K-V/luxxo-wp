The Experience

Embark on a curated journey through "God's Own Country." This carefully designed 5-day itinerary offers a perfect blend of rich history, breathtaking hill station scenery, and the iconic backwater experience. Discover the colonial charm of Kochi, breathe in the crisp air of Munnar's sprawling tea estates, and drift peacefully along the serene waters of Alleppey in a traditional houseboat.

Day by Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Kochi

Arrive at Cochin International Airport. Our representative will greet you and transfer you to your hotel. Spend the afternoon exploring Fort Kochi, visiting the Chinese Fishing Nets, St. Francis Church, and wandering through the historic streets of Jew Town. Enjoy a traditional Kathakali dance performance in the evening.

Day 2: Kochi to Munnar

After breakfast, embark on a scenic drive to Munnar, a picturesque hill station famous for its tea plantations. Check into your resort and spend the rest of the day at leisure, enjoying the cool mountain air and panoramic views.

Day 3: Munnar Sightseeing

Explore the natural beauty of Munnar. Visit the Eravikulam National Park, home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr. Enjoy a guided tour of a tea museum to learn about the history of tea cultivation in the region. Stop by the Mattupetty Dam and Echo Point for stunning photo opportunities.
